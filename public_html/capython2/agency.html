Welcome to Blüme.co.

We plan. We Promote. We Blüme.

We believe that digital communication should, above all, bring people together. We are a professional social media agency, yet deeply accessible and always open to your ideas. Our goal is simple: to bring a true breath of fresh air to your online presence.

What we do

We offer comprehensive, tailored support to help your brand bloom on social media. Our expertise covers:

  • Strategy

    Defining your presence across various social platforms.

  • Content Creation

    Designing engaging posts, stories, and promotional ads.

  • Digital Identity

    Building a strong, cohesive social identity that truly reflects who you are.

Logo

The idea behind the logo was to represent the authentic and natural connection between our clients and us. We chose a flower to illustrate how an idea can grow in one's mind and bloom with our help.

Who we do it for

Whether your project is big or small, our doors are open. We collaborate with:

  • Associations and foundations
  • Businesses (of all sizes)
  • Individuals with a vision

Our approach and values

To us, social media is about much more than algorithms; it's a human journey. Our approach is centered on authentic and natural connection. We make it a priority to champion inclusion, diversity, and respect for the unique identity of every single client.

Colors, fonts and textures

We chose natural colors to reflect the organic aspect of both the flower and our name. These three accent colors reflect the agency's personality. With a simple and clean palette, the goal is to make the logo easily recognizable.

The font choices were made to differentiate between the different types of text, using a clean and simple body font to ensure readability.

The textures reflect both the natural and media aspects of the agency.
