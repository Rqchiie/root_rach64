NGE.3.2 — Complete my part of the work

Level 10

I can highly prioritize all team meetings. When I cannot attend, I call the team to ensure I produce high quality products for which I am responsible. I complete my work on time or early, make sure my team members are aware of our deadlines, and encourage them to meet them. I can provide criteria-based feedback to my team members.

Evidence

  1. Setting up the project on Figma

    After creating the agency and event site, I placed all the links on Figma so the whole team could access them easily.

  2. Daily check-ins

    In person, I always repeated what we had to do for the next day or asked each person what they were finishing. This kept everyone on track.

  3. Asking for and giving feedback

    I always asked for feedback — on the event site and logo design. I also gave feedback when needed, like to Shyra about the event logo.

  4. Leading the final product

    I communicated what still needed to be done and led a major part of the deliverable: both the agency site and the event site.
