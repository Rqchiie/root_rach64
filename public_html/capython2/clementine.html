Capyskills · NGE.3

NGE.3 Collaborate Effectively - I can work effectively with diverse teams to create high quality products.

Level 8

I can brainstorm ideas to manage and resolve a conflict. I can work with others to determine which solution will provide the most benefit to all involved in the conflict and I can help to implement the solution. I can take responsibility for my role in the conflict.

Evidence

  1. Group Brainstorming Session

    Before starting the project, I encouraged a group brainstorming session so we could openly discuss our ideas and agree on a shared direction.

  2. Organizing Tasks

    To avoid any conflicts regarding the workload, I took responsibility for organizing our tasks by creating a Trello board and dividing our Figma workspace part by part with everyone's names.

  3. Video script

    I wrote the video script and asked every member to check their part to see if it suited them. I then made small changes based on their feedback.

  4. Group Vote for Logo Design

    When we had multiple different proposals for our logo, I proposed a group vote to ensure the final choice benefited everyone.

  5. Instragram Group Chat

    To implement clear communication, we created an Instagram group chat together, and I initiated regular discussion moments to track our progress at each stage of the project.
